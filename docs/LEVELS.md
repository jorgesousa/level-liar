# Levels (spoilers)

Every level title is a promise, and every promise is a lie. The truth appears in the HUD after your first death on that level.

| # | Promise | Truth | What actually happens | How to beat it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Just walk to the door. | Mind the floor. | A stretch of floor drops as you approach. | Jump the hole as it opens. |
| 2 | Nothing to see here. | Spikes. Everywhere. | Spikes pop up where you land after the gap; later a pair slides towards you. | Jump the gap early, then wait for the sliding spikes to stop. |
| 3 | The door won't move. | It's shy. | The door hops to the far left. On the way back the floor drops and spikes pop up. | Turn back, jump the hole, jump the spikes onto the door. |
| 4 | Solid floor. Safe ceiling. | Neither. | A spiked pillar falls into the pit, then sinks if you stand on it. A spiked ceiling slowly comes down. | Wait for the pillar to land, cross without stopping, keep moving under the ceiling. |
| 5 | Every platform is solid. | Some of them crumble. | The middle platform crumbles, the last one rises, spikes pop up where you land. | Hop straight off the crumbling one and jump from the very edge of the last. |
| 6 | Controls work as expected. | Left is right. Sometimes. | Left and right swap halfway, then swap back. | Let go when the screen glitches and re-think your thumbs. |
| 7 | No moving parts. | It comes back. | A saw rises from the floor, rolls at you, then turns around faster. | Jump it twice. |
| 8 | The exit is to the right. | Look up. | The floor under the exit drops away; stairs to the real exit appear. | Stop at the sign, then climb. |
| 9 | Take your time. | Don't stop. | Ceiling spikes drop as you pass under them. | Keep running, jump the gap without slowing down. |
| 10 | Last level. Honest. | That one was actually true. | The exit turns into spikes and the whole floor collapses behind you. | Climb the stairs that appear, fast. |
