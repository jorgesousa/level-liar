# Levels (spoilers)

Every level title is a promise, and every promise is a lie. The truth appears in the HUD after your first death on that level.

| # | Promise | Truth | What actually happens | How to beat it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Just walk to the door. | Mind the floor. | A stretch of floor drops as you approach. | Jump the hole as it opens. |
| 2 | Nothing to see here. | Spikes. Everywhere. | Spikes pop up where you land after the gap; later a pair slides towards you. | Jump the gap early, then wait for the sliding spikes to stop. |
| 3 | The door won't move. | It's shy. | The door hops to the far left. On the way back the floor drops and spikes pop up. | Turn back, jump the hole, jump the spikes onto the door. |
| 4 | Solid floor. Safe ceiling. | Neither. | A spiked pillar falls into the pit, then sinks if you stand on it. A spiked ceiling slowly comes down. | Wait for the pillar to land, cross without stopping, keep moving under the ceiling. |
| 5 | Every platform is solid. | Some of them crumble. | The middle platform crumbles, the last one rises, spikes pop up where you land. | Hop straight off the crumbling one and jump from the very edge of the last. |
| 6 | Controls work as expected. | Left is right. Sometimes. | Left and right swap halfway, then swap back. | Let go when the screen glitches and re-think your thumbs. |
| 7 | No moving parts. | It comes back. | A saw rises from the floor, rolls at you, then turns around faster. | Jump it twice. |
| 8 | The exit is to the right. | Look up. | The floor under the exit drops away; stairs to the real exit appear. | Stop at the sign, then climb. |
| 9 | Take your time. | Don't stop. | Ceiling spikes drop as you pass under them. | Keep running, jump the gap without slowing down. |
| 10 | Last level. Honest. | That one was actually true. | The exit turns into spikes and the whole floor collapses behind you. | Climb the stairs that appear, fast. |
| 11 | The door is the exit. | The door has other plans. | The door runs across the pit, climbs to a high ledge, then charges at you and dies in the spikes. A spare appears. | Jump it when it charges, then take the spare. |
| 12 | Walls keep you in. | There are no walls. | The screen wraps left–right and top–bottom; the floor drops after you wrap. | Run off the right edge, fall through the floor, land on the ledge by the door. Jump the spikes that pop up. |
| 13 | Gravity is constant. | Jump flips it. | Jump flips gravity. The door moves to the ceiling. | Flip between floor and ceiling to dodge the spike rows. |
| 14 | The floor is not slippery. | Ice. Obviously. | Ice physics, one fake floor tile, a conveyor belt that pushes you back. | Short hops, brake in the air. |
| 15 | Size doesn't matter. | It does. Constantly. | Pads shrink and grow you; the door shrinks into a mouse hole. | Small for the tunnel, big for the wall, small again for the hole. |
| 16 | Portals take you forward. | Portals take you somewhere. | One portal's exit wanders over a spike pit; the "EXIT" portal sends you back to the start. | Wait for the exit to be over solid floor; dodge the EXIT portal. |
| 17 | You can't fly. | You can. You shouldn't. | Flappy flight through pipes; the door is a painting; the real one appears when flight ends. | Hover between pipes, land on the platform, touch the painting, drop to the real door. |
| 18 | Checkpoints save your progress. | You have to die to win. | The checkpoint respawns you upside down on the ceiling, where the door is. | Grab the flag, die on purpose. |
| 19 | Up is up. | Not on this screen. | The camera turns upside down and sideways mid-jump. | Trust the platforms, not the picture. |
| 20 | This is the final level. | It was. Honest. | A warp in front of the door, a fake THE END, the floor collapses, then rising credits. | Run back to THE END, ride the credits up, jump to the ledge. |
